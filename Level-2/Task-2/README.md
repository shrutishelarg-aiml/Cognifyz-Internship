# Level 2 - Task 2: Price Range Analysis

## Overview

This task focuses on analyzing restaurant price ranges and their relationship with customer ratings.

## Objectives

- Determine the most common price range.
- Calculate the average rating for each price range.
- Identify the rating color associated with the highest average rating.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Analysis Performed

- Examined the distribution of restaurant price ranges.
- Calculated average ratings for each price category.
- Analyzed rating colors and their average ratings.
- Created visualizations to support findings.

## Key Findings

- Price Range 1 is the most common category.
- Higher price ranges generally have higher average ratings.
- Dark Green represents the highest average rating category.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows a relationship between restaurant pricing and customer ratings, with premium restaurants tending to receive higher ratings.
