# Level 2 - Task 1: Table Booking and Online Delivery

## Objective

Analyze the availability of table booking and online delivery services among restaurants and examine their relationship with ratings and price ranges.

## Tasks Performed

- Calculated the percentage of restaurants offering table booking.
- Calculated the percentage of restaurants offering online delivery.
- Compared average ratings of restaurants with and without table booking.
- Analyzed online delivery availability across different price ranges.
- Created visualizations to support the analysis.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## Key Findings

- 12.12% of restaurants offer table booking.
- 25.66% of restaurants provide online delivery.
- Restaurants with table booking have higher average ratings.
- Online delivery is most common in mid-range restaurants.

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that table booking services are associated with higher customer ratings, while online delivery availability varies significantly across price ranges.
